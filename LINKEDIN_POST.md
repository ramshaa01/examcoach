Just built and deployed ExamCoach during [Hackathon Name/Weekend]! 🚀

Indian students preparing for high-stakes exams like UPSC, JEE, and NEET face immense pressure. A major gap in their preparation is personalized, step-by-step coaching at scale. Generic question banks just don't cut it anymore.

That's why our team built **ExamCoach - A Personalized AI Study Agent**. 🎓

It's not just a chat interface. We designed a true Multi-Agent System using the Google Antigravity architecture concept and Gemini 1.5. 

Here’s the flow:
1️⃣ **Question Agent**: Generates highly targeted exam-level questions dynamically.
2️⃣ **Evaluation Agent**: Grades answers, provides step-by-step guidance, and classifies the *exact* type of mistake (conceptual vs calculation). 
3️⃣ **Weakness Tracker**: Maintains a persistent student profile to ensure you never get stuck on the same weak concept twice.
4️⃣ **Knowledge RAG Agent**: Deeply grounded on actual PDFs (NCERTs & study notes) via FAISS, ensuring no hallucinations.

Built with an elegant Streamlit UI and a FastAPI backbone, the system intelligently routes workflows and adapts the difficulty based on historical learning. And it's fully containerized for Google Cloud Run! ☁️
